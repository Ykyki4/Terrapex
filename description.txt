Terrapex adds a full progression to Terraria: seven tiers, four bosses and two mini-bosses,
running from your first cave to the far side of the Moon Lord.

The world cracked long before you got here. The crack is not an event, it is a substance -
it crystallises into ore, grows dust, grows creatures, and eventually grows an eye to keep
watch over itself. Every tier is a question about how many of them are looking at you.

CREATURES
- 4 bosses, one per tier from pre-hardmode to post-Moon Lord
- 2 rare mini-bosses that nobody summons; you just meet them
- 16 enemies

144 ITEMS
- 38 weapons: 12 melee, 8 ranged, 7 magic, 6 summon staves, 5 whips
- 7 armour sets, 37 pieces in all - the top four tiers carry four heads each, one per class
- 22 accessories, including 2 pairs of wings
- 13 materials, 12 consumables, 5 tools, 2 kinds of ammunition
- 13 blocks and furniture: ore, torches, lanterns, trophies, banners
- 1 mount, 1 pet

AND AROUND THEM
- 102 recipes and 2 crafting stations of the mod's own
- 16 buffs and debuffs
- Its own ore, with world generation
- All art is hand-drawn pixel work

This is an early release. Bug reports and balance complaints are wanted - especially from
multiplayer, which has had far less testing than single player. There is no custom music yet;
the bosses use vanilla tracks.
