'''Weapons''' in [[Terrapex]] cover all four damage classes at every one of the mod's seven tiers. Every tier offers at least one melee weapon, one ranged, one magic and one summon or whip, so no class is ever left behind a gate.

Damage figures below are the base values before any modifier, difficulty scaling or armour bonus.
