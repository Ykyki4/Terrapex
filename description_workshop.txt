[h1]Terrapex[/h1]

[b]Seven tiers, four bosses, two mini-bosses and 144 items[/b], running from your first cave to the far side of the Moon Lord.

The world cracked long before you got here. The crack is not an event, it is a substance — it crystallises into ore, grows dust, grows creatures, and eventually grows an eye to keep watch over itself. Every tier of this mod is a question about how many of them are looking at you.

[b]Wiki:[/b] [url=https://ykyki4.github.io/Terrapex/]ykyki4.github.io/Terrapex[/url] — every item, enemy, buff and recipe, plus class setups for all seven tiers. Also in [url=https://ykyki4.github.io/Terrapex/ru/]Russian / на русском[/url].

[h2]The bosses[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]The Dormant Eye[/b] — pre-hardmode, after the Eye of Cthulhu. A boulder with a lid grown shut over it. Four plates, and the lid splits exactly once.
[*][b]Keeper of the Rift[/b] — post-Wall of Flesh, in the emptiest stretch of vanilla progression. Three phases, eight destructible plates, thirteen attacks.
[*][b]The Weaver of the Rift[/b] — post-Plantera. A loom that walks. It drives its own legs into the ground as anchors and strings the arena between them; the weapon is not a projectile, it is the room.
[*][b]The First Keeper[/b] — post-Moon Lord. It does not aim at you. It [i]looks[/i], and the looking is the attack: stand in its cone of sight and you both hurt each other twice as easily. Its eight lids are a dial you can turn in either direction.
[/list]

[h2]How the fights are built[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Every attack is announced.[/b] An eye flash, a wind-up, a drawn aim line, a thread pulling tight — something you can read before anything can hurt you.
[*][b]Attack order is a fixed rotation per phase, never random.[/b] These are bosses you learn, not bosses you react to.
[*][b]Nothing a boss fires homes.[/b] Volleys are laid on where you were standing when they left, so leading a shot is a skill the fight actually asks for.
[/list]

[h2]Armour that does something[/h2]
From the third tier on, every set has [b]four heads — one per class[/b] — that share a mechanic and then rewrite what it is for. The sets differ in kind rather than by a number: a shell of orbiting plates that eats enemy shots, a stitch that joins two enemies so hitting one hits the other, a loom that plants anchors in the arena and strings your own thread between them, and a cone of regard that pays you for facing your problem instead of kiting it.

[h2]And the rest[/h2]
[list]
[*]15 enemies and 2 rare mini-bosses that nobody summons — you just meet them.
[*]Weapons for all four classes at every tier, plus accessories, wings, a mount and a pet.
[*]Its own ore with world generation, two crafting stations, furniture and trophies.
[*]All art is hand-drawn pixel work.
[/list]

Bug reports and balance complaints are wanted, [b]especially from multiplayer[/b] — it has had far less testing than single player. There is no custom music yet; the bosses use vanilla tracks.
